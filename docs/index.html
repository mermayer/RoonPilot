04

From first boot to Roon control

A clean factory image contains no private Wi-Fi or Roon data.

  1. 1

    Join the setup AP

    Connect to RoonPilot-Setup-XXXXXX. If the captive page does not open, visit 192.168.4.1.

  2. 2

    Connect Wi-Fi

    Select or enter your 2.4 GHz network and save the password locally on the device.

  3. 3

    Open the local UI

    Use the address shown by your router or the device. No Internet account is required.

  4. 4

    Approve RoonPilot

    In Roon, open Settings → Extensions and enable RoonPilot by Senior Coder.

  5. 5

    Select the server

    If several Roon Servers are found, choose the intended one. A fixed local IP can be entered when discovery is blocked.

  6. 6

    Choose your zones

    Select the primary zone and hide rooms that should not appear on the device.

Wrong Wi-Fi password? After about 45 seconds RoonPilot returns to its setup AP, so the credentials can be corrected without erasing or reflashing.

05

Built for the way you listen

Fast physical control, a considered round interface and complete local configuration.

Tactile control. Beautifully simple.

Turn the full outer ring for volume. RoonPilot automatically follows native percent, dB and relative outputs. Tap, swipe or hold the capacitive display for playback and device controls.

  • Play/pause, previous and next on the display
  • Horizontal swipe for track changes
  • Three selectable now-playing layouts
  • Zone picker, native percent/dB volume and Roon-pairing screens
  • Station and digital clocks with day/night brightness
  • Quick settings, optional control lock and centre double-tap display-off

Turn · tap · double-tap · swipe · hold

Every gesture at a glance.

Volume, transport, zone selection, Quick Settings, control lock, immediate display-off and wake-up are available directly on the device.

Web interface. Total local control.

The responsive local interface manages RoonPilot directly from the same network.

  • Roon Server and zone management
  • Display, rotary and touch settings
  • Configuration export/import without secrets
  • Signed online and local A/B firmware updates
  • Visible update status with separate web/device notification controls
  • Diagnostics and battery runtime calibration
  • Guarded deep sleep with touch and rotary wake

No bridge. No Raspberry Pi. No additional service.

RoonPilot talks directly to Roon on your local network. Configuration, pairing and control remain local.

06

One precisely supported module

RoonPilot targets the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Knob-Touch-LCD-1.8, not a generic round ESP32 display.

Main processor
ESP32-S3R8 · 16 MB flash · 8 MB PSRAM
Display
1.8-inch round IPS LCD · 360 × 360 · capacitive touch
Controls
Full rotary ring plus touch gestures
Companion
ESP32-U4WDH · independent 4 MB flash
Onboard hardware
PCM5100A DAC, microphone, vibration motor and microSD
Power
USB-C or optional internal 3.7 V / 800 mAh battery

Blue and black enclosure variants are sold with or without an included battery. Check the exact order code and regional battery restrictions before purchasing.
